Eye-anchored viewmodel, entity yaw/model wiring. The first-person gun now renders yaw-only at a view-space forward push (`VIEWMODEL_FORWARD = 24`) instead of at the eye with full view angles: the software backend drops (not clips) alias tris touching the near plane, so the eye anchor ate the breech half of real v_weap meshes and blanked the gun entirely at pitch -30 (measured headless on pak0.pak v_blast). The push keeps the whole baked-composition mesh visible across ±30° pitch, stays centered at any yaw (no orbit), and never inherits pitch/roll swing. HostDriver gains three optional, backwards-compatible hooks — `entityAngles(id)`, `entityModel(id)`, `extraEntities()` — so drivers can propagate per-entity yaw (monsters face the player), per-kind model/skin handles (soldier vs gunner vs bolt vs barrel), and snapshot-omitted entities (bolts, barrels, explosions); absent hooks fall back to the legacy hardcodes (angles 0,0,0, model/skin 0, no extras). Frozen `Refresh`/`Entity`/`RefDef` contracts untouched. Tests: viewmodel origin/angles vs camera at pitch 0/-30 and yaw sweep, yaw/model propagation + defaults, extras rendering + no-hook green path.
